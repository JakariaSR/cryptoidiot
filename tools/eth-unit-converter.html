ETH Unit Converter (Ether ↔ Gwei ↔ Wei)

Make sense of gas fees once and for all

Every Ethereum gas fee is quoted in gwei — but what is a gwei? It's just a smaller unit of Ether: 1 ETH equals 1 billion gwei, and 1 gwei equals 1 billion wei (the smallest unit).

Once the units click, gas fees stop being mysterious. This converter translates between Ether, Gwei and Wei instantly — the same math your wallet does before every transaction.

Frequently asked questions

What is Gwei?

Gwei (gigawei) is a unit of Ether equal to 0.000000001 ETH. Gas fees are priced in gwei because typical fees are tiny fractions of an Ether.

What is Wei?

Wei is the smallest unit of Ether, named after Wei Dai. 1 ETH = 1,000,000,000,000,000,000 Wei (10¹⁸). Almost no one quotes fees in wei directly.

How many Gwei in 1 Ether?

1,000,000,000 (1 billion) gwei. So a gas price of 20 gwei equals 0.00000002 ETH.

Why are gas fees in Gwei and not ETH?

Because gas fees are small amounts. Saying a fee is '0.000021 ETH' is awkward; '21,000 gas at 1 gwei' — or just quoting gwei — is far more readable.

Is this converter accurate?

Yes — the conversion is pure mathematics (powers of 10), so it's exact every time, no rates or rounding involved.
