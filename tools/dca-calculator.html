Frequently asked questions

What is DCA in crypto?

DCA (dollar-cost averaging) means investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals regardless of price. You buy more when prices are low and less when they're high, averaging out your cost over time.

Is DCA a good strategy for Bitcoin?

Historically, DCA into Bitcoin has smoothed out volatility for long-term holders. It won't beat buying at the perfect bottom, but almost nobody can time the bottom consistently.

What growth rate should I use?

For a conservative simulation, many people use 5–10% annually. Crypto is volatile, so treat any projection as an illustration, not a prediction.

Does the calculator include fees?

No — exchange fees and recurring-buy fees are not included. On a real exchange, small per-purchase fees will slightly reduce returns.

Is this financial advice?

No. The calculator is an educational tool that illustrates how dollar-cost averaging works mathematically. Always do your own research.
