Sats Converter (BTC ↔ Satoshis)

Bitcoin is divisible — 1 BTC = 100,000,000 sats

One Bitcoin costs thousands of dollars, but you don't need to buy a whole one. Each Bitcoin is divisible into 100 million units called satoshis (or sats), after Bitcoin's creator Satoshi Nakamoto.

Thinking in sats makes small purchases feel concrete: instead of owning 0.001 BTC, you own 100,000 sats. This converter flips between both units instantly and shows the live dollar value.

Frequently asked questions

What is a satoshi?

A satoshi is the smallest unit of Bitcoin, named after its creator Satoshi Nakamoto. 1 satoshi = 0.00000001 BTC.

How many satoshis are in 1 Bitcoin?

100,000,000 satoshis. That's 100 million, which is why whole Bitcoin prices of tens of thousands still allow very small purchases.

Why do people count in sats?

Because owning '50,000 sats' feels more tangible than owning '0.0005 BTC'. Sats also make it easier to compare small purchases and track stacking progress.

Can I buy just a few satoshis?

Practically, exchanges have minimum order sizes well above a few sats — usually a few dollars' worth. But technically, yes: Bitcoin is divisible to 1 sat.

What's the live USD value based on?

The USD estimate uses the current Bitcoin price from CoinGecko, refreshed on each page load.
