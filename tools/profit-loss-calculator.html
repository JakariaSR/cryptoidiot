Frequently asked questions

How is crypto profit calculated?

Profit = (sell price − buy price) × number of coins. Our calculator derives the coin count from your investment and buy price, then applies the same formula.

Does the calculator include fees and taxes?

No — it shows the raw profit. Real-world returns are reduced by exchange fees, withdrawal fees and (depending on your country) capital gains tax.

What if I bought at different prices?

For multiple purchases at different prices, you'd calculate the average buy price first (a DCA calculator handles that scenario better), then plug the average into this tool.

Why does percentage matter more than dollars?

Percentage normalizes for position size — a $100 gain means very different things on a $200 investment versus a $20,000 one.

Is this tool free?

Yes — it runs entirely in your browser, with no signup and no data stored anywhere.
