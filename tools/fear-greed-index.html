One number that captures the market's mood

Markets are driven by emotion as much as logic. The Fear & Greed Index distills the crypto market's emotional state into a single number from 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed).

It combines volatility, market momentum, social media chatter, surveys and Bitcoin dominance into one daily reading — a useful gut-check before you make any move.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Fear & Greed Index?

A 0–100 scale that measures crypto market sentiment, created by alternative.me. Low values mean fear, high values mean greed.

How is the index calculated?

It combines six factors: volatility, market momentum/volume, social media sentiment, surveys, Bitcoin dominance, and Google Trends data for crypto queries.

What should I do when the index shows extreme fear?

Many contrarian investors see extreme fear as a potential buying opportunity, since panic selling often pushes prices below fair value. It's a signal to think, not an automatic buy order.

What does extreme greed mean?

Extreme greed often coincides with market tops and corrections. Historically, when everyone is euphoric, risk is usually highest.

How often is the index updated?

The Crypto Fear & Greed Index is updated once per day, based on data from the previous 24 hours.
