How to use this tool

  1. Enter the amount you'd stake, in dollars.
  2. Enter the APY (annual percentage yield) — typical major-coin staking rates are roughly 2–7%.
  3. Set the duration in months.
  4. Enter a price-change scenario for the coin over that period — 0% shows pure staking rewards, negative values show what a price drop does to the total.
  5. Click Calculate to see rewards earned, final value, and total return.

Frequently asked questions

What is APY in staking?

APY (annual percentage yield) is the yearly reward rate for staking a coin, including compounding. A 5% APY means your staked amount would grow by roughly 5% over a year — paid in the staked coin, not dollars.

Does this calculator include the coin's price change?

Yes — that's the point. The price-change field lets you see the final dollar value of your stake plus rewards under any scenario, so you can compare '5% APY but coin down 20%' against just holding cash.

Are staking rewards guaranteed?

No. Real APYs move with network conditions, and risks like slashing or validator downtime can reduce rewards. Treat any APY as a variable rate, not a promise.

How is the calculation done?

Rewards compound monthly using the standard APY formula: value = amount × (1 + APY)^(months/12). That final coin amount is then priced at your chosen price-change scenario.

Are staking rewards taxable?

In most countries, yes — rewards are usually taxed as income when received, and any gain when sold is a separate capital event. The math in this tool ignores taxes; your real net return will be lower depending on your country's rules.
