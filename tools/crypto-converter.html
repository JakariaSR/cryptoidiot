Frequently asked questions

Is this crypto converter free?

Yes, completely free with no signup, no limits and no ads on the tool itself. It runs in your browser.

Where do the prices come from?

Rates come from CoinGecko, one of the most widely used crypto data aggregators, aggregated from hundreds of exchanges.

How often are rates updated?

The converter fetches fresh rates every time the page loads. Between loads, values may drift slightly from the exact market price.

Which cryptocurrencies are supported?

Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), BNB, XRP, Dogecoin (DOGE), Cardano (ADA) and Litecoin (LTC) — the coins most beginners actually hold.

Does the converter include exchange fees?

No — it shows the raw market rate. Real exchanges add a small spread or fee, so the price you pay will usually be slightly different.
