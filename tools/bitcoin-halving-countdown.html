Bitcoin Halving Countdown

Time remaining until the next halving, updated every second

Roughly every four years, the reward Bitcoin miners receive for adding a new block is cut in half. This event — the halving — is written into Bitcoin's code and will continue until around 2140, when the last of 21 million bitcoins is mined.

The next halving, expected around April 2028 at block 1,050,000, will reduce the new supply of Bitcoin from 900 to 450 BTC per day. Scarcity goes up; new supply goes down — that's the whole point.

Frequently asked questions

When is the next Bitcoin halving?

The next halving is expected around April 2028, at block 1,050,000. The exact date depends on how fast blocks are mined, which averages 10 minutes but varies.

What happens during a halving?

The reward miners earn for each new block is cut in half — from 3.125 BTC to 1.5625 BTC. This halves the rate at which new Bitcoin enters circulation.

Why does the halving matter?

It reduces new supply. Combined with steady or growing demand, that scarcity is why many people watch halvings closely as potential long-term price catalysts.

How many halvings have there been?

There have been four halvings: 2012, 2016, 2020 and 2024. The 2028 halving will be the fifth.

Does the price always go up after a halving?

Historically, significant price appreciation followed the first four halvings over the following 12–18 months. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
